SI today

Private by nature.
Long-term by choice.

Stahlschmidt Incorporated is a private family holding company founded by Uwe T. Schmidt.

Its history spans international trading, supply chain management, executive management services, technology, and investment. Today, SI preserves the family’s corporate heritage and holding-company interests.

Optimum Holdings was established as the family office to manage the family’s interests and those of a few other high-net-worth family offices.
