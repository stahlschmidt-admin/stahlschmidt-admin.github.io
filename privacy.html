Privacy

Discretion,
by design.

This website presents selected family and corporate history. It does not provide access to confidential family-office, investment, or client information.

The enquiry form prepares an email in the visitor’s own email application. SI receives the supplied name, email address and/or phone number, business interest and optional message only when the visitor sends that email. These details are used to respond personally to the enquiry; submitting an enquiry does not subscribe the visitor to a mailing list. The form does not automatically send messages or record link taps. Archive search and filtering run in the visitor’s browser without submitting search text to SI.

External publication and archive links lead to other websites, which have their own privacy practices. Fonts may be requested from Google Fonts. Website delivery and access services may process technical information according to their own policies.
