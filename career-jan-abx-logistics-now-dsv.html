Jan-Oliver Schmidt · 1997–2008

ABX LOGISTICS · now DSV

  • Director, Project Management (North America) · ABX LOGISTICS (USA) Inc. · Houston, Texas · February 2005–August 2008. Regional restructuring, P&L, team leadership, tendering, sales, project execution and chartering.
  • Director, Project Development (Asia-Pacific) · ABX LOGISTICS Asia-Pacific · Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia · December 1999–January 2005. Established the regional Project Development Group and helped develop the global Purchase Order Management System.
  • General Manager · ABX LOGISTICS (Malaysia) Sdn. Bhd. · Kuala Lumpur · December 1999–January 2005. Managed 130 people across eight offices, including P&L, budgeting, marketing, personnel, IT and operations.
  • Project Manager · ABX LOGISTICS (Singapore) Pte. Ltd., formerly Thyssen Haniel Logistics · Singapore · May 1997–November 1999. Led industrial project logistics, including the Fluor Daniel / INCO nickel plant expansion in Sulawesi, Indonesia.

When the e-commerce market changed, Stahlschmidt Incorporated (SI) acquired KPMG’s rights in KeyCommodities.com and put the technology to work in physical trade. When Thyssen and later Olympic Steel exited particular U.S. trading activities, the family acquired interests and continued building the business. MAN’s international metals activities were consolidated into Ferrostaal Metals Group and later combined with CCC Steel. The Nippon trading partnership developed into the BridgeTrade venture, whose remaining interest MetalsBridge acquired in 2022. After Solar Trust of America’s original operating chapter ended, its name and brand were preserved and revived in 2021 to house the family’s significant solar investments. The enterprise continued through changing markets, ownership structures and technologies.
