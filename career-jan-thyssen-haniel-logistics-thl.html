Jan-Oliver Schmidt · 1995–1997

Thyssen Haniel Logistics (THL)

  • Delegate, Levant Region · Thyssen Haniel Logistic International GmbH · Damascus, Syria · May 1996–May 1997. Established and managed the representative office, negotiated the Syriamar agency agreement, and supported Syrian and Lebanese projects including Beirut International Airport.
  • Management Trainee · Thyssen Haniel Logistic GmbH · Düsseldorf, Germany · April 1995–May 1996.
  • Project logistics rotation · Thyssen Haniel Logistic International GmbH · Bremen, Germany · May 1996.
  • Sea-freight import/export operations and sales · Thyssen Haniel Logistic (España) SA · Barcelona, Spain · January–April 1996.
  • Air-freight, express services and controlling · Thyssen Haniel Logistic International GmbH · Frankfurt, Germany · October–December 1995.
  • Inland waterway/coastal shipping and chartering · Haeger & Schmidt GmbH · Duisburg, Germany · July–September 1995. Managed the Germany–UK river/coastal service during staff absences.
  • Heavy-lift, special cargo, international road transport, warehousing and IT · Thyssen Haniel Logistic GmbH Rheinkraft · Duisburg, Germany · April–June 1995.

When the e-commerce market changed, Stahlschmidt Incorporated (SI) acquired KPMG’s rights in KeyCommodities.com and put the technology to work in physical trade. When Thyssen and later Olympic Steel exited particular U.S. trading activities, the family acquired interests and continued building the business. MAN’s international metals activities were consolidated into Ferrostaal Metals Group and later combined with CCC Steel. The Nippon trading partnership developed into the BridgeTrade venture, whose remaining interest MetalsBridge acquired in 2022. After Solar Trust of America’s original operating chapter ended, its name and brand were preserved and revived in 2021 to house the family’s significant solar investments. The enterprise continued through changing markets, ownership structures and technologies.
