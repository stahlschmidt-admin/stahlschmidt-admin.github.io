HCC Holdings (HCCH)

A 50/50 joint venture with Reinhard Seifert, founder of Hanseatic Coal & Coke Trading GmbH (HCC), Hamburg, established to supply 100% of the solid-fuel requirements of the state-owned Železara Smederevo steelworks in Serbia.

The five original wholly owned divisions

Distinct capabilities.
Shared enterprise.

The original operating divisions were sold. Their names and work remain part of the Stahlschmidt legacy.

Stahlschmidt Asset Management
SAM

SAM was the hands-on investment unit. It participated directly in the management and operations of portfolio businesses across steel, oil and gas, renewable energy, e-commerce, and property management in the United States and internationally.

Its approach included businesses managed by Stahlschmidt Executive Services, promising ventures, and companies needing early venture capital or later cash or tax equity. The family’s principle was to do well financially while doing good.

Sold

Original division page ↗
Stahlschmidt Executive Services
SES

SES went beyond identifying problems and proposing solutions: its executives implemented them through tailored management assignments. Its “performance on demand” approach sometimes tied part or all of its compensation to results, in cash or stock.

It specialized in market entry, crisis management, and corporate diplomacy, with engagements in steel, conventional and renewable energy, infrastructure, e-commerce, and real estate. Client assignments remained confidential unless a client authorized disclosure or the engagement was already public.

SES was sold and subsequently operated as Bridge Consulting LLC.

Sold

Original division page ↗
Stahlschmidt Investment Group International
SIGi

SIGi was the international investment unit, distinct from SAM’s direct operational management. It invested in publicly traded securities and in private companies at an early stage, seeking rights alongside their founders.

Its historical areas included steel, oil and gas, renewable energy, e-commerce, general aviation, and property management in the United States and abroad. It favored proven management teams, strong prospects aligned with global trends, potential public offerings, and the family’s principle of doing well while doing good.

Sold

Original division page ↗
Stahlschmidt Online Solutions
SOS

Started in 1999, SOS led Stahlschmidt’s e-commerce and information-management work. Through KeyHoldings, it developed KeyCommodities.com as a free metals marketplace, together with KeyOffset, KeyTrack&Trace, and other tools for physical steel trading and distribution.

In 2000, SOS entered a Zurich venture with KPMG Fides to develop the platform. When the e-commerce market turned and KPMG withdrew, SOS acquired KPMG’s intellectual-property rights and systems, then put the platform to work in physical steel trading and supply-chain operations. It also backed and mentored early online ventures.

Sold

Stahlschmidt United Network
SUN

SUN was among the five original wholly owned divisions. Its activities were later incorporated in Optimum Holdings.
